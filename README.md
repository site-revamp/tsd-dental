# TSD Dental Group — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

TSD Dental Group is a Singapore-based dental healthcare provider operating six clinic branches across Choa Chu Kang, Hougang, Tampines, and Yishun. The group provides general and specialist dental care, including preventive dentistry, restorative treatments, orthodontics, dental implants, and oral surgery.

The redesigned homepage focuses on creating a modern, welcoming, and patient-centred digital experience that highlights accessible dental care, simplifies treatment discovery, and makes clinic selection and appointment enquiries more convenient.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Messaging and Patient Guidance

**Previous Issue:** The original homepage introduced the dental group and its services but did not bring together its patient benefits, same-day dental-pain messaging, and appointment actions prominently enough.

**Redesign Improvement:** Strengthened the hero section with clearer patient-focused messaging, supporting information, and prominent appointment actions. The existing same-day dental-pain message receives greater visibility, helping visitors understand the practice's offering and identify their next step more quickly.

### 2. Improved Treatment Discovery and Navigation

**Previous Issue:** Although the original homepage introduced the group's broad range of dental services, visitors needed a clearer and more visual way to identify relevant treatments.

**Redesign Improvement:** Organised key dental services into a structured, easy-to-scan treatment overview with clearer service descriptions and navigation pathways. This helps patients explore preventive, restorative, orthodontic, and specialist treatment options without unnecessary browsing.

### 3. Simplified Multi-Clinic Discovery and Appointment Access

**Previous Issue:** With six clinic branches, visitors needed a more connected way to identify suitable locations, access branch-specific contact information, and proceed with appointment enquiries.

**Redesign Improvement:** Prioritised clinic discovery through a clearer location-selection experience featuring branch-specific contact and appointment actions. Improved mobile navigation makes it easier for patients to find a convenient clinic and contact the appropriate team.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign focuses on several targeted visual and usability enhancements:

- **Clearer Visual Hierarchy:** Established a more direct progression from the main introduction to treatment options, clinic discovery, and appointment actions.
- **Stronger First Impression:** Highlighted patient benefits, accessible dental care, and the existing same-day dental-pain message.
- **Improved Service Presentation:** Organised dental treatments into a more visual, structured, and accessible overview.
- **Enhanced Trust Communication:** Reinforced the practice's patient-focused values, including quality care, professional relationships, and accessibility.
- **Simplified Clinic Navigation:** Improved the presentation of the six branches and their contact pathways.
- **Mobile-Friendly Experience:** Prioritised responsive layouts, readable information, and convenient appointment access on smaller screens.
- **Consistent Visual Presentation:** Strengthened spacing, typography, content organisation, and overall design consistency.

## Summary

The redesigned TSD Dental Group homepage addresses three primary usability and communication challenges: insufficiently focused first-screen messaging, limited treatment discoverability, and complex multi-clinic navigation.

The improvements establish a clearer, more welcoming, and accessible patient journey while reinforcing the group's commitment to quality dental care, patient relationships, and convenient appointment access across Singapore.
